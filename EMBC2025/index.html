LocNeXt:
Fast Automatic ConvNeXt-based Coronary Ostia Landmarks Detection in 3D CCTA Images

by Matteo Leccardi

Thank you for participating!

You will be showed images from a CT scan of the heart. Blood with contrast liquid appears clear. Please select where the indicated coronary ostium is.

The image below is a slice of a CT image of an anonymized patient with contrast-liquid injected in the arterial blood stream. Images of anonymized patients are taken from the publicly available CAT08 and ASOCA datasets.
The flashing green overlay is the aorta, while the red one is the right coronary artery.

The image below is another slice of a CT image, usually anatomically lower (closer to the feet of the patient).
The flashing green overlay is again the aorta, while the blue one is the left coronary artery.

As you can see, the interface between the green aorta and the red/blue coronary arteries is blurred.
Your task will be to place a single point where you think the middle/center of the interface is, a.k.a. where the coronary artery begins.

Please select the right coronary ostium
